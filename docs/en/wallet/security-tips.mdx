# Wallet Security Tips

Ring Platform combines platform credit balances with optional on-chain wallets. Follow these practices to avoid scams and account loss.

## Verify the domain

- Only sign in at your organization's official Ring URL (for example `ring-platform.org` or your white-label domain).
- Check the lock icon and HTTPS in your browser before entering credentials or approving payments.
- Bookmark the real site — do not follow links from unsolicited email or DMs.

## Never share secrets

- **Ring staff will never ask** for your password, recovery phrase, private key, or one-time codes.
- Do not paste seed phrases or export files into chat, forms, or "verification" pages.
- Wallet export and backup flows (when available) run only inside your authenticated profile after re-verification.

## Payment and transfer safety

- Confirm recipient addresses character-by-character before sending on-chain RING.
- For store checkout, verify order totals and merchant name on the checkout screen before paying with credit balance.
- If a deal pressures you to pay outside the platform, treat it as a red flag.

## Report suspicious activity

- Use **Contact** or your admin channel to report phishing sites impersonating Ring.
- If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password and review active sessions under **Profile → Security**.
